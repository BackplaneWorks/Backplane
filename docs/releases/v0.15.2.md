# Backplane 0.15.2

0.15.2 makes Backplane faster when the computer is busy, fixes ordering from start to finish and smooths out many first-day annoyances. It also brings Japanese to the desktop app and Google accounts to bots. If you run 0.15.1 or 0.15.0, it offers this update; your threads and settings carry over as they are.

This is the first release we tested end to end with **Claude Fable 5.2**. We gave five Fable 5.2 testers a fresh Backplane each and a goal in one sentence, such as "design a USB-C lamp dimmer and get it ready to order", "find every feature", "run a small shop with bots and routines" or "use it on a starved machine", and no instructions on how to use the app. They found the annoyances a new user hits, fixed most of them themselves and sketched ideas for the rest. It was very helpful, and most of the fixes below come from that night.

## Faster, especially on a busy computer

- **Replies stream in as they're written.** The desktop showed a streaming reply in jumps every five seconds; it now updates up to ten times a second, and only in the window showing that thread.
- **Typing and opening threads stay quick.** The hub now does far less work for each picture it sends the window. On a large home, one key press went from about 75 ms of hub work to about 7 ms, and the round trip from 78 ms to 10 ms. A long pasted log or reply in a thread no longer slows every key press in it.
- **Long replies can't hog the hub.** Streamed steps are paced by how busy the hub is, so a long reply on a starved machine uses about half the CPU it did.
- The sidebar is built once per update instead of once per project.

## Ordering

- **Buy it works from start to finish.** An order thread now gets all of its instructions, so it runs the review checks and asks about quantity and country before exporting the files.
- **The Buy it button appears as soon as the design is ready to order**, not only after reopening the window.
- **Diff no longer drops the connection** after an order has exported its manufacturing files.

## First day

- **Setup's first project and first thread work.** Choosing an existing folder now adds it (it used to do nothing and say nothing), and setup ends in your first thread with the message box ready.
- **Add project accepts a pasted path.**
- **New threads get sensible titles.** About half of them used to be named after the wrong part of the first message.
- **Board, Schematic and 3D show a design as soon as it appears.** A viewer opened before the project's first board or schematic used to stay empty.
- The rail's letter keys (B, S, 3, …) work from the Board and Schematic views, as the tour and tooltips promise.
- Pictures in a reply fit a narrow thread column.

## Bots, rooms and routines

- **A bot answers in the room you asked in**, not only in its own chat.
- **Routines understand "every 20 minutes", "every 2 hours", "hourly" and "daily at 09:30"**, and Edit shows the schedule in those words instead of a code.
- Ctrl+K finds rooms and bots by name.
- **Gmail and Calendar for bots.** Add Google accounts in Settings → Accounts → Google and choose which bots may use them; sending mail and changing the calendar ask for your approval first.

## Viewers

- **Selecting a trace selects its whole net**, and probing works between the schematic and the board again.
- The 3D views have a background that follows the theme, or a colour of your own (Settings → This device), and a lighter scene.
- The Browser tab runs Chromium in a window of its own inside the app on Linux, Windows and macOS.

## Everything else

- **Japanese in the desktop app** (Settings → This device → Language), and on Android and iPhone (Settings → Language). Agents' text and file names stay as they are.
- A thread's cost and tokens update during a turn, not only when it ends.
- Links in messages open: threads, projects, rooms, files and web pages. Nested lists show their levels.
- The message box grows with its text when a queued message sits above it.
- Diff hides Commit when nothing changed, says "Commit & push" for what it does, and explains in one line when git doesn't know who you are.
- History names a shell step by its command.
- `&sonnet` and the like offer the newest model first.
- Tooltips use the app's font. Renaming a thread keeps the box on one row.
- "That is not on this hub" now says what happened: the thread was deleted, or it is on another machine.
- A bundled skill for designing laser-cut sheet parts and preparing them for ordering.

## Installing

- **Linux and macOS:** `curl -fsSL https://github.com/BackplaneWorks/Backplane/releases/download/v0.15.2/install.sh | sh`, or press **Update** in the app.
- **Windows:** extract the zip and open `backplane-gpui.exe`, or press **Update** in the app. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the exe is not signed yet).
- **Android:** install `backplane-v0.15.2-android.apk`. It installs over 0.15.1.
- **iPhone:** through TestFlight.

## Known issues

- **Windows:** the programs aren't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen warns on first start. Managed PCs may not allow running them.
- **Japanese on Linux** needs a CJK font installed (for example Noto Sans CJK).
