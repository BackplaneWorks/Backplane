# Backplane 0.15.3

0.15.3 is 0.15.2 with one change: anonymous usage counts are back. 0.15.0 to 0.15.2 kept your Share anonymous usage choice but never sent anything, because the part that sends was left behind when the hub was rebuilt. If you run 0.15.2, 0.15.1 or 0.15.0, it offers this update; your threads and settings carry over as they are.

## Anonymous usage counts

**What is sent:** installs, updates, daily activity, and daily totals of threads, messages, model tokens, bot traffic and boards ordered. Each comes with a random install id, the version, the system and the day.

**What is never sent:** your conversations, projects, files, names, paths or keys.

Sharing is **on unless you turn it off**, and nothing is sent until Backplane has told you about it:

- **A new install:** setup's Anonymous usage step says what is sent, with **On** already chosen. Choose **Off** there to send nothing. Sending starts once you finish setup.
- **An existing install:** a one-time notice appears at the top of the app, on desktop, iPhone or Android, whichever you open first. It has **Turn off** and **Settings** buttons. Sending starts once the notice has been shown.
- **If you already chose Off** (in 0.14 or 0.15), it stays off. You won't see the notice and nothing is sent.

You can change it at any time in **Settings, Privacy**. Reset id gives your install a new random id. The details are in [docs/telemetry.md](../telemetry.md).

## Installing

- **Linux and macOS:** `curl -fsSL https://github.com/BackplaneWorks/Backplane/releases/download/v0.15.3/install.sh | sh`, or press **Update** in the app.
- **Windows:** extract the zip and open `backplane-gpui.exe`, or press **Update** in the app. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the exe is not signed yet).
- **Android:** install `backplane-v0.15.3-android.apk`. It installs over 0.15.2.
- **iPhone:** through TestFlight.

## Known issues

- **Windows:** the programs aren't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen warns on first start. Managed PCs may not allow running them.
- **Japanese on Linux** needs a CJK font installed (for example Noto Sans CJK).
