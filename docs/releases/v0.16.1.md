# Backplane 0.16.1

0.16.1 brings the new Backplane KiCad (bp.13.1) to every computer, which puts KiCad in the pane on the Mac and brings KiCad's standard libraries and Backplane's own to Windows and Mac. It also fixes the issues 0.16.0 listed. If you run 0.16.0 or 0.15, it offers this update; your threads and settings carry over as they are.

## Backplane KiCad bp.13.1

When you press **Edit** next, a row above the editor offers the update, with what it brings and its size: **Update**, or **Not now** (asked again a day later). It reopens on the new version by itself unless you have unsaved changes. Settings → CAD offers it too.

- **On a Mac, KiCad opens in the pane,** as on Linux and Windows.
- **KiCad's standard libraries and Backplane's own** come with it on Windows and Mac, registered on a fresh install and added to your existing tables on update. A library you removed stays removed.
- **No more OpenGL box on Windows** computers without OpenGL 2.1 graphics (some virtual machines and remote desktops): KiCad draws in software by itself and says so in the pane.
- **In a narrow pane, KiCad's side panels fold away** so the board keeps most of the room; they come back at full size.
- **Fixed in the embedded editor:** on Windows, Ctrl+S after a click on the canvas; on a Mac, paste, undo, and Cmd+O opening another project inside the pane; on every computer, the "Incomplete undo/redo operation" box.

## Fixes

- **If you turned anonymous usage Off in 0.14, it stays Off.** Importing a 0.14 home used to lose that choice and ask again. A home already imported that way is put back to Off once, before anything is sent, when the 0.14 home still says Off and you haven't chosen On since.

## Installing

- **macOS** needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 13 or later; Backplane KiCad needs macOS 15 or later. Intel Macs aren't supported.
- **Linux and macOS:** `curl -fsSL https://github.com/BackplaneWorks/Backplane/releases/download/v0.16.1/install.sh | sh`, or press **Update** in the app.
- **Windows:** extract the zip and open `backplane-gpui.exe`, or press **Update** in the app.
- **Android:** install `backplane-v0.16.1-android.apk`. It installs over 0.16.0.
- **iPhone:** through TestFlight.

## Known issues

- **Windows:** the programs aren't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen warns on first start.
- **macOS:** the app isn't notarized yet. Installed with the command above it opens normally; if you unpack the archive by hand, open Backplane once with right-click → **Open**.
